# Public demonstration data

Companies, recipients, requests, grants and adoptions here are fictional scenarios. Public sources document historical Social Capital backing, not current holdings or product participation.

## NOAA / Saildrone
`saildrone_1045_2021-09-30.csv`: 1,440 observations plus a units row. https://data.pmel.noaa.gov/pmel/erddap/tabledap/sd1045_hurricane_2021.html . CC0. Acknowledge NOAA AOML and PMEL. Source metadata states No QC Performed. Missing wind values and sparse wave sampling do not establish sensor outages.

## NREL PVDAQ
`pvdaq_system10_sample.csv`: 1,440 observations, system 10, January 1, 2015. Public NREL/PVDAQ data, **not Palmetto customer data**. https://data.openei.org/submissions/4568 . Deline et al., DOI https://doi.org/10.25984/1846021 . CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Original fixture preserved; deliberate faults are applied to in-memory copies. Local timestamps have an unresolved timezone convention. Negative AC power is retained, not declared erroneous.

Company sources: https://www.saildrone.com/about and https://palmetto.com/about/media-center/press-release/palmetto-raises-375-million-to-accelerate-residential-clean-energy-independence . Names are illustrative context, not endorsements.
